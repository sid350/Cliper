# Cliper Privacy Policy

Cliper does not collect, transmit or share any data.

- Cliper has no analytics, tracking, advertising or crash-reporting code.
- Cliper does not access the network.
- Your clipboard history is stored only on your Mac (in the app's container in ~/Library) and is never sent anywhere.
- Content that password managers mark as concealed or transient is not saved.
- You can delete individual items or clear the entire history at any time from the app.

The source code is public: https://github.com/sid350/Cliper

Contact: open an issue at https://github.com/sid350/Cliper/issues
